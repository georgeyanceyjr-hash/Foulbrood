FoulBrood GUI

Play, explore positions, and review games with your choice of Hive engine.

Start here
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Drag FoulBrood GUI Beta.app into Applications.
  3. Double-click the app. It opens the viewer in your default browser.

No Python, developer tools, or account is needed. This download is for Macs with an Apple M-series chip. Intel Macs and Windows are not supported by this package.

If macOS blocks the first launch

This community beta is not Apple-notarized and has no Developer ID signature. If you trust the download, first try opening it, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm Open. Follow Apple’s instructions. Managed Macs may not allow an exception.

Add an engine

No playing engine is included. Two people can play immediately; computer play and Engine insight become available after you connect an engine.

  1. Open the Engine tab and choose Find engines online.
  2. For an easy first setup, download Mzinga v0.16.0. Choose its MacOSArm64 archive.
  3. Extract it and keep the whole engine folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents/Engines.
  4. In FoulBrood, choose Browse this Mac…, select MzingaEngine inside that folder, then Add & connect.

If you cannot locate the extracted engine, try Find engines on this computer. The viewer supports UHP engines only. An engine may have its own macOS first-launch approval or installation requirements.

Things to try

  • Play: human versus human, human versus engine, or engine versus engine; clocks, move suggestions in the player bars, and separate sound volumes.
  • Position: analyze a position and watch suggestions directly on the board.
  • Game: import a PGN/JSON game, select a supported engine and thinking time, then compare played and preferred moves, sort by move difference, and navigate the history. Use Save review and Load review to keep the results.
  • Setup: arrange a position or bring one in from the current game or analysis.
  • Engines: connect several engines and choose among them in the player menus and Engine insight.

Saving and closing

Use Save review to keep a game with its review results, or export an ordinary game. Games and reviews are not saved automatically across a backend restart. Stop analysis or game review and pause play before closing the viewer tab; active reviews can continue in the background. A small local helper remains running so reopening the app can resume the session. Logging out or restarting the Mac ends it.

The viewer runs locally. Internet is needed for engine downloads and importing a public game link. Engine paths and cache files are kept separately from the development version under ~/Library/Application Support/FoulBrood GUI Beta. Downloaded engines remain in the folders you chose.

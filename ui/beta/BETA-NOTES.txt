FoulBrood GUI — 0.1.0-beta.3
Apple Silicon Mac beta · September 16, 2026

Included: viewer, Hive rules helper, Python runtime, local assets and licenses.
Not included: any selectable playing engine, personal engine paths, test games,
accounts, or developer tools. FoulBrood is not required as a playing engine.

CHANGES IN BETA 3
• Engine information now appears in player bars above and below the board.
• White suggestions are gold; Black suggestions are blue. Recommendations follow
  your selected notation and the History option for board flips and rotations.
• Game review compares played and preferred moves, shows their difference, and
  lists moves by largest or smallest difference with history navigation.
• Save review and Load review preserve analysis in a PGN that still opens as a
  normal game in other compatible Hive programs. Interrupted reviews can resume.
• Imports and setup changes warn before replacing history or review work.
• Recorded in-game analysis can be revisited and transferred into game review.
• Longer analysis times and Match timing for Play; improved clocks and sound.
• Auto board fitting, less clutter, and a warmer light-mode board background.

KNOWN LIMITS
• Apple Silicon only. Built with a macOS 11 minimum; tested on macOS 15.6.1
  with Chrome, plus focused Safari and Firefox checks. Older macOS versions
  and a separate clean Mac still need tester verification. External engines set their own requirements.
• No Apple notarization or Developer ID signing. Local ad-hoc signatures provide
  integrity checks, not Apple approval. See START-HERE.html for first launch.
• Use Save review to keep a reviewed game, or export an ordinary game. Automatic
  game/review recovery after a backend
  restart and automatic updates are not included.
• Stop analysis/review and pause play before closing the viewer. A local helper
  remains running after closing the browser; active reviews can continue. Log out or
  restart your Mac to end it completely. Before replacing this beta with an
  update, export your games, close the viewer, and log out/back in.
• Raw scores from different engines use different scales. Game review labels
  are estimates from the selected engine and thinking time, not objective truth.
• Game review supports the verified Mzinga and Nokamute score formats. Other
  UHP engines can play; review is enabled only if a recognized score format
  passes the viewer's compatibility checks.
• A forced-win distance is shown only when the engine provides a recognized
  forced result. An ordinary large score is not treated as proof of a win.
• An external engine's executable and companion files must stay in place.
  If you move them, remove the old entry and add the engine from its new folder.
• Keep one viewer tab open per session to avoid duplicate audio and competing
  controls. The default-browser launch is local to this computer, not a hosted site.

FIRST TEST ON ANOTHER MAC
1. Extract and launch without installing Python or developer tools.
2. Record any macOS approval messages and whether Open Anyway works.
3. Play several human moves, including a placement and a piece movement.
4. Connect the Mac ARM64 Mzinga release and play a short game against it.
5. Export/import the game; try a short review, stop and resume it, then use
   Save review and Load review to check that its results return.
6. Check sounds, board previews, setup, and reopening the app.

Please send reproducible steps, a screenshot and a PGN/JSON when relevant.
Do not send personal files or an entire engine cache. FEEDBACK.txt is a template.
